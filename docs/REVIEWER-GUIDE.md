# IBD Assessment app — guide for the reviewing gastroenterologist

**Link:** https://ibd-scoring.netlify.app/

This is a **draft for clinical review**. The scores and warning thresholds are proposed defaults taken from published sources and guidelines. Please don't use it for real patients yet. Your comments decide what changes.

## 1. Install it on your phone (about 30 seconds)

### iPhone
1. Open the link in **Safari**. Chrome and Viber/Messenger in-app browsers can't install apps on iPhone. If the link opened inside a chat app, tap **⋯** or the compass icon and choose **Open in Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**. An **IBD Assess** icon appears on your home screen.
5. Open it from that icon. It runs full-screen like a normal app.

### Android
1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮** (top right), then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Tap **Install**.

Once installed, the app opens even without signal. Changes we make appear automatically the next time you open it online.

## 2. Try it (5–10 minutes)

1. On **Home**, tap one of the **demo cases**, e.g. *Acute severe UC*, *Crohn's with possible obstruction* or *Suspected toxic megacolon*. These are made-up patients.
2. The app jumps to the **triage summary**: emergency warnings first, then physiological instability, severe activity, surgical and infection flags, the scores and the missing investigations.
3. Tap **How this was calculated** under any score to see each item and its points.
4. Tap the steps at the top (**Patient · Exam · Scores · Vitals · Labs**) to change answers. The summary updates immediately.
5. Tap **Create report**, then **Save / share PDF** to get the report as a PDF (send it to yourself, save to Files or Drive).
6. You can also tap **Assess** and enter your own made-up case from scratch.
7. Try the three **themes**: tap the **Theme** button (top right) or use **Choose a look** on Home. **Soft** is the original look, **ER** is flat and high-contrast like hospital ER systems, **ER Night** is the dark version. Tell us which works best for the ER (there's a 💬 Comment button there too).

## 3. Leave feedback

- Tap **💬 Comment** under any score or warning to note what should change: a threshold, wording, a missing item, something that should or shouldn't trigger.
- Use the **Feedback** tab for general comments.
- Each comment is **sent to the app team automatically**. Without signal, it waits and sends as soon as you're back online. The **Feedback** tab shows ✓ Sent or Not sent yet for each one.
- Optional: **Share a copy** on the Feedback tab sends all your comments by Messages, Viber, WhatsApp or email.
- Please don't type real patient details into comments.

## Useful things to look at

- Do the warning thresholds match your practice? Tachycardia > 100 / > 120, SBP < 90, CRP > 30 mg/L for acute severe UC, and the paediatric pathway under 18. They are listed under **Feedback → Settings and local protocol values**.
- Is the line under every emergency warning ("Inform the responsible doctor now, per local protocol") worded as you want?
- Should nurses and interns enter exam findings (guarding, tenderness, abdominal mass)? Currently they can, with a "Not assessed" option.
- Which theme should be the default for nurses and interns?
- Anything missing from the ER workflow you'd expect?

## Good to know

- **Nothing about patients is saved:** not on the phone, not on a server. Closing the app clears the assessment. Only your feedback comments are kept on your phone until you clear them.
- The app gives **no triage category** and makes **no admission, discharge or treatment recommendation**.
