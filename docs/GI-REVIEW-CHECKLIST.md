# Gastroenterologist Review Checklist

**For:** the supervising gastroenterologist (sign-off reviewer)
**Spec version:** 0.1.0-draft · **Prepared:** 2026-11-05

The app will not calculate scores or show emergency warnings until this checklist is signed. For each item, tick **Accept** (use the proposed default) or write your decision. Details are in `IBD-CLINICAL-RESEARCH.md` (question IDs match §8) and `IBD-SCORING-SPECIFICATIONS.md`.

Please also check each scoring table in the research doc against the original paper. They were verified only through secondary sources, because PubMed, PMC and Springer were not reachable when they were written.
